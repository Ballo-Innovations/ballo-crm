Pipeline board — 3 options

Drag any card to another stage (or focus a card and press ← →). Column counts and totals update and a toast offers Undo. Each card is one opportunity; clicking it would open the drawer.

Same in every option: columns are the scoring stages (Qualify · Discover · Propose · Negotiate; Won/Lost via the drawer or by dropping on a Won/Lost bar); dropping on a closed stage asks for confirmation (and a reason for Lost); totals are in kwacha, weighted = value × stage probability; on phones columns snap one at a time and you swipe between them; drag also works with the keyboard and screen readers (dnd-kit).
