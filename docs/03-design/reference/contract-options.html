Contract stepper + signature — 3 options

A contract for Kariba Power Co. Press Next step to move it along. At Sign, draw a signature with your mouse or finger (or switch to Type); Sign turns on once there is a signature. Reset starts over.

Same in every option: steps are Draft → Sent → Viewed → Signed → Active; each records who and when (the audit trail); signing needs a drawn or typed signature plus the consent line; the signed PDF is stamped with the signature, name, time and IP; a declined or expired contract shows the step in red with a reason; the signer can sign from a phone (the pad fills the width).
