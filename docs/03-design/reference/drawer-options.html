Drawer — 3 options

The drawer is where every detail view opens (opportunity, account, lead, score explanation). Click any row to open the drawer, switch tabs, try ⋯ and the buttons; close with ✕, Esc or by clicking outside. Switch device and theme to compare.

A

Floating card

A rounded card floats over the page with a 12px gap on three sides and a soft dim behind it. Matches the rounded panel language most closely. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet you can drag down.

+ Most on-brand, feels light+ Clear focus− Covers the list behind it
B

Docked side panel

The drawer slides in as a white column inside the grey panel and the list shrinks to make room. No dimming — you can keep clicking rows and the drawer updates. On phones it takes the whole panel.

+ Work through a list fast (row → row)+ Nothing hidden− Squeezes wide tables
C

Wide sheet with black summary header

A wider sheet attached to the right edge. The top is a black band with the key numbers (value, score, stage) — the same "one strong black element" idea as the highlighted KPI. On phones it goes full screen.

+ Key numbers jump out (great for demos)+ Room for tables and timelines− Heaviest; covers most of the page
Shared in every option: ✕ and ⋯ in the header, tabs (Overview · Score · Activity · Contacts), a sticky footer with the actions, Esc closes, focus stays inside while open, the selected row stays highlighted.
