Forms — 6 options

The same "New lead" form (as it would appear in the drawer) in six input styles. Everything works: type, tab through, pick from dropdowns, enter a value (it formats as kwacha), and press Create lead with empty fields to see errors.

AFilled

Soft grey fields with no border, like the search box. On focus the field turns white with a black outline.

BOutlined

White fields with a thin grey border, the classic form look. On focus the border goes black with a soft halo.

CPill, floating label

Rounded pills; the label sits inside when empty and floats up and out above the field when you click or type.

DUnderline

No boxes at all — just a line under each field and small caps labels. The airiest, most editorial look.

EInline labels

Label on the left, value on the right, rows divided by hairlines — like a record sheet. Densest; reads well as an editable detail view.

FTiles

Each field is its own white tile on the grey, label tucked inside. Echoes the dashboard cards; easy to tap on phones.
