# Risk Register

This register tracks known risks to BalloCRM's delivery, security, and quality during and after
the 2026 iHub Innovation Hackathon. It is a living document — update it whenever a new risk is
identified, a mitigation is implemented, or a risk's likelihood/impact changes. See also
[decisions.md](./decisions.md) for the architectural choices referenced in several mitigations,
and [sprint-plan.md](./sprint-plan.md) for when each sprint boundary review happens.

**Likelihood/Impact scale:** Low / Medium / High.

## Risk review cadence

This register is reviewed and updated at every sprint boundary alongside [sprint-plan.md](./sprint-plan.md)
planning — new risks discovered during a sprint are added immediately rather than held for the
next review, but likelihood/impact/mitigation status for existing risks is formally reassessed
at each sprint close-out. Closed or fully mitigated risks are struck through with a note (date
and mitigating change/PR) rather than deleted, so the register also serves as a historical record
of what could have gone wrong and didn't.
